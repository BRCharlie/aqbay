# iGaming CRM

## Overview

Aqbay provides an iGaming Customer Relationship Management (CRM) solution designed to help casino operators, sportsbook platforms, and sweepstakes businesses manage player engagement, marketing campaigns, loyalty programs, and operational workflows from a centralized platform.

The CRM integrates with gaming platforms to provide insights into player activity, promotional performance, customer segmentation, and retention strategies.

---

# What is an iGaming CRM?

An iGaming CRM is a customer relationship management system specifically developed for online gaming businesses. It helps operators organize player data, automate marketing activities, monitor player behavior, and improve customer retention.

Aqbay's CRM is designed to work alongside casino software, sportsbook platforms, and sweepstakes casino solutions.
