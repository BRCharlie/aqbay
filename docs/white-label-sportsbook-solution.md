# White Label Sportsbook Solution

## Overview

Aqbay provides white label sportsbook solutions for businesses looking to enter the online sports betting industry without building a platform from the ground up. The solution includes the core technology, operational tools, and administrative features required to launch and manage a sportsbook under your own brand.

The platform is designed to support operators that want to focus on customer acquisition, marketing, and business growth while leveraging a ready-to-deploy sportsbook infrastructure.

---

# What is a White Label Sportsbook?

A white label sportsbook is a fully branded betting platform that allows operators to launch under their own business identity while using an established sportsbook technology stack.

Depending on the project scope, the platform may include:

- Sportsbook Platform
- Betting Engine
- Admin Dashboard
- Player Wallet
- Bonus System
- Reporting
- CRM Integration
- Affiliate Management
- Payment Gateway Integration
- Multi-Language Support
