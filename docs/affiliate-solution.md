# Affiliate Solution

## Overview

Aqbay provides an Affiliate Solution that helps casino operators, sportsbook platforms, and sweepstakes businesses manage affiliate partnerships, referral programs, commission structures, and performance tracking from a centralized platform.

The solution enables operators to monitor affiliate performance, automate commission calculations, and gain insights into acquisition campaigns through real-time reporting and analytics.

---

# What is an Affiliate Solution?

An affiliate solution is a software platform that allows gaming operators to collaborate with marketing partners who refer new players to their platform. The system tracks referrals, player activity, and commissions while providing transparency for both operators and affiliates.

Aqbay's Affiliate Solution is designed to integrate with casino software, sportsbook platforms, and sweepstakes casino solutions.
